# The example: controlled unclassified information

The application every adapter is measured against: a document store under the marking rules of 32 CFR Part 2002, the controls of the CUI Registry, and thirteen rules, C1 to C13, that `docs/example.md` §3 states. The domain, its contexts, and every scenario live here; a thin application (`example_rbac`, `example_postgres`, `example_cerbos`, `example_fga`) binds one adapter to it and adds only what that adapter needs. This package has no application callback and starts nothing.

- The schemas. `Example.Domain.Document`, `Example.Domain.Portion`, `Example.Domain.Marking`, `Example.Domain.Proposal`, `Example.Domain.Program`, `Example.Domain.Assignment`, `Example.Domain.OfficeRole`, `Example.Domain.Agency`, `Example.Domain.Office`, and `Example.Domain.Category` declare an object type with `use Turnstile.Schema`, and the facts and relationships a rule reads. A Document carries its Marking, its Portions, and its Proposals, so a decision on the document admits queries and nested writes of the rows it carries. `Example.Domain.User`, `Example.Domain.AccountRole`, and `Example.Domain.OverrideReport` are the account, the override permission, and the override's report.
- `Example.Application.Documents`: the reads and the marking writes. `read/3` asks the port for `read` and returns the document with its banner (C1, C2); `read_redacted/3` asks for `read_redacted` on the document and `scope` over its portions, and returns the portions the subject may read (C4); `list/2` is `scope` over documents. `change_marking/4`, `set_decontrol/4`, and `decontrol/3` are C7 operations, refused by the port without a designator role and a fresh session (C8), and a banner that would admit a subject a portion denies is refused in the domain (C4). `change_portion_marking/4` recomputes the banner in the same transaction. `override_read/4` is C10: a privileged subject with the permission and a justification reads under a declared exemption, emits `[:example, :override, :read]`, and is reported to the designating office.
- `Example.Application.Proposals`: separation of duties (C9). `propose/4` needs `propose_marking` on the document; `approve/3` needs `approve_marking` on the proposal, which the adapters give to an approver other than the proposer, and applies the marking as a nested write under the proposal's decision.
- `Example.Application.Accounts` and `Example.Domain.Sessions`: the roles an account holds, written under a declared exemption because role administration is not a rule of the example, and the re-authentication window (C8) the adapters that compute in code compare against `reauthenticated_at`.
- `Example.Domain.Controls`: the four control kinds and the fields a marking carries, which every schema that holds a marking reads its values from.
- `Example.Domain.Banner`: rule C4 at the domain's face: a row's marking, the banner over a set of rows, and whether a banner covers a marking, which is the test a marking change is refused by. A banner admits no subject a portion denies: its categories and controls are the union of the portions', and its releasable-to list is the intersection of the lists of the portions that carry that control. It reads markings as values and holds nothing, so a property covers the laws rather than examples.
- `Example.Siem`: the consumer of the library's three events. Each decision, change, and access event becomes an OCSF record of schema version 1.3.0, held in memory. `core/ocsf.ex` carries the class, the activity, the severity, and the product metadata, which are the consumer's to choose because they move between schema versions; the event payload carries the rest. `docs/events.md` §6 has the mapping.
- `Example.Application.Review`: the access review report, who may do what on the documents of each agency and every privileged account, from the port's `review`: one rule and one decision per subject under one operation id, each query run under the subject it reports on.
- `Example.Repo` and `Example.OwnerRepo`: the application-role repo every query on a protected schema passes through with a decision or an exemption, and the owner-role repo for migrations and truncation.
- `core/document_query.ex`, `core/account_query.ex`, and `core/review_query.ex`: the queries the contexts run, apart from the calls that run them, so what a context asks for reads on its own and a query can be built without a database.
- `Example.Migrations.Domain`: the domain tables as a helper the thin application's first migration calls; no foreign key cascades into a fact schema, so a revocation deletes nothing but the fact.
- `test/support`: `Example.Fixture`, the world every scenario starts from; `Example.Scenarios.Table`, the scenario table of `docs/example.md` §4 as data, held to the document by a freeze test; `Example.Scenarios`, every scenario declared once, with its body in a module named by its group, and defined in a thin application's one test file by `use Example.Scenarios`, which ends by checking that the ids defined are the table's ids, all of them and no others.

The package's own tests cover the contexts under `Turnstile.Test.Fake`; the scenarios run in each thin application, under the adapter it binds, and never here. The bind cost of each thin application, in lines of policy and glue, is in its README.
